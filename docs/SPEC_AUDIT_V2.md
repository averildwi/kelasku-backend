# Spec Audit V2 — KelasKu (re-audit after revision)

Re-audit of `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`, `docs/API_SPEC.md` against the skills, after the 24 findings of `docs/SPEC_AUDIT.md` were addressed. The fourth doc in `docs/` (`FOLDER_STRUCTURE.md`) was also checked for stale references.

## 1. Verdict

**FIX FIRST**

One blocker remains: the whiteboard **update** permission contradicts itself three ways (PRD §2 table vs PRD F-WB-1 vs API_SPEC). The audit decisions settled **delete** only. Two majors: the API_SPEC conventions bullet still names the old `Role` enum and lists `WALI` as a per-class role value, and `FOLDER_STRUCTURE.md` still describes an architecture (refresh tokens, `@Public()`, role in the JWT, Socket.IO) that the final specs and the skills explicitly forbid.

1. **Who may UPDATE a whiteboard note?** PRD §2 says WALI/KETUA/BENDAHARA (any note), F-WB-1 and the API_SPEC PATCH say uploader only, the API_SPEC matrix WALI cell says any note. (The audit decision settled delete only.)
2. **`/auth/me` profile field name after the rename:** `globalRole` (matching `User.globalRole`), and is the register body field `role` or `globalRole`?
3. **Should `FOLDER_STRUCTURE.md` be archived/rewritten?** It still specifies refresh tokens, `@Public()`, a role-bearing JWT and a Socket.IO gateway — all forbidden by the final specs and the skills.
4. **Restore of a LEFT member:** which status/message (`409 "was not kicked — re-join via code"` is the proposal)?
5. **Where does the shared push sender live:** `src/notifications/` (exported service) or `src/common/notifications/`?
